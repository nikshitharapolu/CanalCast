CanalCast is a scenario simulator comparing Panama and Suez routes under user-selected Panama Canal restrictions. It is not a forecast.

Python computes every displayed number. Explanations must describe the current calculated results.

Every model input must have a value, unit, source or assumption rationale, and status: sourced, approximate, assumption, or derived. Derived values must identify their inputs and method.

Missing required values must remain missing. Never replace them with invented numbers or zero. Disable the affected calculation and explain what is needed.

Show fuel cost per voyage and per loaded TEU. Do not call this total shipping cost. Tolls, insurance, and other unmodeled costs are excluded and disclosed.

Show fuel-combustion CO₂, not lifecycle emissions.

Distinguish actual transits from booking-slot limits, containers from TEU, and historical observations from modeled scenarios.

No network calls are required at runtime. Use local data. Missing optional historical series must not prevent the simulator from running.

Do not show shipping-line names or logos; use Carrier A/B/C if carrier examples are added.

Expose assumptions and data dates clearly. Historical replay does not constitute model validation.
